                    ┌─────────┐

  Legend:
    DRAFT     = employee edits freely
    SUBMITTED = waiting for manager
    LOCKED    = approved; structure frozen
    RETURNED  = same as draft for employee


================================================================================
              CALENDAR — WHAT IS ACTIVE WHEN
================================================================================

  Month     │  Portal mode for most users
  ──────────┼──────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Before May│  Goal setting: CLOSED (or preview only)
  May       │  ● GOAL SETTING OPEN — create, submit, approve
  Jun       │  Usually closed until July (buffer)
  July      │  ● Q1 CHECK-IN OPEN
  Aug-Sep   │  Closed / read-only until October
  October   │  ● Q2 CHECK-IN OPEN
  Nov-Dec   │  Closed until January
  January   │  ● Q3 CHECK-IN OPEN
  Feb       │  Closed until March
  Mar-Apr   │  ● Q4 / FINAL ACHIEVEMENT OPEN

  Admin can configure exact dates; app blocks wrong actions.

================================================================================
              PROGRESS SCORE — QUICK DECISION TREE
================================================================================

  When employee saves actual, system asks:

  What UoM was this goal?

      Numeric/% and HIGHER is better?  →  score = Actual ÷ Target
      Numeric/% and LOWER is better?   →  score = Target ÷ Actual
      Timeline (deadline date)?        →  score from date vs deadline
      Zero-based (0 = good)?           →  Actual=0 → 100%, else 0%

  Show % on employee & manager screens (tracking only).

================================================================================
              EXAMPLE WALKTHROUGH (STORY)
================================================================================

  1. Priya (Employee) logs in May 3.
  2. Adds 4 goals: Revenue 30%, NPS 25%, TAT 25%, Training 20%.
  3. App shows "100% — ready to submit". She submits.
  4. Raj (Manager) sees Priya "Pending". Tweaks TAT target, approves.
  5. Priya's sheet is LOCKED.
  6. July: Priya enters Q1 actuals; status "On Track". Score auto-shows 78%.
  7. Raj opens Priya's Q1 view, writes check-in comment, saves.
  8. HR (Admin) exports July report: planned vs actual for all staff.
  9. October → repeat steps 6–7 for Q2.
 10. Admin unlocks one goal for Priya after policy change — audit log records it.
